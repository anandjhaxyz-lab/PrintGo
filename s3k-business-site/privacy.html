CUSTOMER INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026. This policy explains how S3K – Print Yourself handles information when you use its self-service document printing service.

1. Information processed

To provide a print service, the kiosk may process the document or image you choose to upload, your selected print settings, a kiosk/session reference, and technical information needed to operate and protect the service. If you contact support, we may also process the details you choose to provide.

2. How information is used

  • To receive and process your selected document for printing.
  • To calculate and display the order amount and selected print options.
  • To confirm transaction and print-job status where online payment is enabled.
  • To respond to support requests, investigate service issues and prevent misuse.
  • To meet applicable legal, accounting and regulatory obligations.

3. Uploaded documents

Upload only documents you are authorised to print. Documents may contain personal or confidential information. They are processed for the printing service and may be retained for the time needed to complete the service, investigate a reported issue, protect the service or meet legal obligations. Access should be limited to authorised service operations. Do not upload documents you do not want processed for printing.

4. Payments

When Cashfree checkout is enabled for a transaction, payment processing is handled through the Cashfree payment service. S3K may receive payment status, order references and transaction information needed to reconcile the print order and provide support. Never share your UPI PIN, card PIN or OTP with service staff.

5. Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers involved in operating the kiosk, hosting the service, processing payments or meeting legal requirements, only as needed for those purposes. We do not publish uploaded documents as public files.

6. Security and retention

Reasonable technical and operational safeguards are used to protect service information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Information is retained only as required for service delivery, security, dispute handling and applicable legal obligations, then deleted or otherwise handled under the applicable retention process.

7. Your questions

For a privacy-related query or a request concerning information you provided, contact the S3K service desk at +91 91774 80140 or through WhatsApp.
