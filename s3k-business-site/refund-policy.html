PAYMENT SUPPORT

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026. This policy explains how S3K – Print Yourself handles payment and printing issues.

1. Before confirming

Please check the document preview, page selection, copies, colour mode, side selection and final amount before confirming payment. If you have not paid, you can leave the checkout flow without completing the order.

2. Payment debited but order not confirmed

If your bank or payment app shows a debit but the kiosk does not show a successful payment or confirmed order, do not repeatedly attempt payment. Contact the service desk and provide the payment transaction reference, approximate time and kiosk/session details. The transaction will be checked against the payment provider's status.

3. Duplicate payment

If you believe you were charged more than once for the same print request, contact support with the relevant transaction references. Confirmed duplicate captures will be reviewed for reversal or refund.

4. Printing failure or incomplete output

If payment is confirmed but the kiosk does not produce the print, or the output is incomplete because of a service or equipment fault, contact support. The transaction and job status will be checked. Depending on the verified issue, an appropriate resolution may include a reprint or a refund.

5. Completed printing and customer-selected files

Once the requested pages have been successfully printed, an order generally cannot be cancelled simply because the wrong file, page range or print setting was selected by the customer. Please review the preview and total before payment. Verified service faults will still be reviewed.

6. Refund processing

Where a refund is approved, it will be initiated through the original payment method or payment provider process wherever supported. The time for the amount to appear in the customer's account depends on the payment provider, bank and payment method.

7. How to request help

Contact the S3K service desk at +91 91774 80140 or through WhatsApp. Include the transaction reference and a short description of the issue. Never share your UPI PIN, card PIN or OTP.
