You are responsible for selecting the correct file and ensuring that you have the right to reproduce it. Do not upload unlawful content or documents that infringe another person's rights. Check the preview before proceeding.

Before confirming an order, review the selected page range, number of copies, colour mode, one-sided or two-sided option and the total amount displayed by the kiosk. 4. Payment and order status

Where online checkout is available, an order is treated as paid only after the payment status is confirmed by the payment provider and the service. Do not make repeated payments solely because a confirmation screen is delayed. Contact support with the transaction reference if the amount was debited but the order status is unclear.

Printing depends on kiosk availability, connectivity, paper and printer operation. If a print is incomplete, incorrect due to a kiosk fault or not produced after a successful payment, contact the service desk with the transaction or session reference so the issue can be checked.
